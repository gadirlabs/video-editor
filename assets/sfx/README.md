# The starter sound set

Five sounds, generated with `sox` from scratch. They are original work with no third-party
rights in them, released under this repository's MIT licence, so nothing here needs
crediting or checking.

| File | Used for |
|---|---|
| `pop.wav` | a small element appearing — one item in a list, a label |
| `tick.wav` | a step in a staged reveal |
| `whoosh.wav` | a full-frame insert arriving, cued 70 ms early so the sound leads the picture |
| `swell.wav` | the open and the close |
| `draw.wav` | a line or connector drawing itself |

They are deliberately plain. They will not make a video sound designed; they will stop it
sounding unfinished, which is the actual job. Point `sfx_dir` in `project.json` at your own
library when you have one.

**Rules that apply to whatever library you use.** Quiet and functional: a sound marks a
change on screen and is otherwise absent, 12 to 20 dB under speech. Vary them — no single
sound more than three or four times in a video, and never twice running. Record what each
video used, so a licence question can be answered later.

**Never script downloads from a subscription library.** Most licences forbid automated
downloading, the account belongs to the person you are working for, and a per-download
licence usually means one use per download. Ask them to download what is needed.

**Never take sounds out of an editor that bundles them.** A bundled library is licensed for
use inside that application, not for export into someone else's video.
